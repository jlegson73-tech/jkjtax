About

Meet Domingo Legson

Domingo Legson founded JKJ Tax & Business Services for people who want a straightforward place to get their finances in order. He works directly with clients, from first-time filers to owners who need books they can trust.

The practice is built around plain language. You should leave a meeting knowing what was filed, what the books say, and what deserves attention next — without a stack of jargon in the way.

Engagements are personal and sized to the work: a single return, ongoing bookkeeping, or a consulting conversation when a decision is coming up.
